    About the FFXIV Crafting Quote Builder

    A free, browser-based crafting quote calculator for FINAL FANTASY XIV. Search any item, and the tool pulls its recipe from XIVAPI and expands every precraft down to base materials, scaling quantities by the recipe yield. Each material is then priced against live market board data from Universalis on the datacenter you choose.

    What it does

    • Recipe and precraft trees — recursive expansion with correct amounts per craft run.
    • Quantity-aware market prices — listings are taken cheapest-first until the amount you need is covered, so a cheap single-unit listing never misrepresents the cost of a bulk order.
    • HQ and NQ — materials are quoted HQ when enough HQ is listed and fall back to NQ otherwise; the finished craft can be switched between HQ and NQ.
    • Buy vs. craft comparison — the cost of buying the finished items outright next to the cheapest material cost of crafting them yourself.
    • Special-source materials — a separate tally of everything that cannot be gathered, crafted or bought from a gil vendor, such as Allagan tomestone and crafters' scrip purchases, aetherial reduction products and drops, so a customer knows what they can supply themselves.
    • Quote sheet — positions with description, quantity and unit price, exported as a PNG image you can post in game or on Discord.

    Who it is for

    Crafters who take commissions and want a defensible price: raid consumables, gear sets, housing items or glamour pieces. Everything runs client-side in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and saved setups stay in your own browser storage.
